Platinum Roadmap — the efficient order

Nine stages, story first, grinds folded into the trips you are already making. Hour ranges are week-one community pace estimates, not promises. Two warnings govern the whole run: the five stranger finales sit in windows tied to story progress, and meditation spots can be lost to story changes — both are called out in their stages below, before the point of no return.

⚠ Point of no return

Before the final mission: finish every stranger strand (Andy, Kenton, Billy, Randy, Tyrone, Bret), sweep the current region's meditation spots, and make a hard manual save in its own slot. The save is your insurance for the choice-driven Actions Speak Louder and the partnership trophy Personal Best — if either has not landed by the credits, reload it instead of replaying the game.
